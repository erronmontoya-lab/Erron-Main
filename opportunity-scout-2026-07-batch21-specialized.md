## The structural finding — why "specialized areas" resist this search

Specialization inverts the visibility of competition: **the more specialized the vertical, the less visible its incumbents are to generalist search — and the more entrenched they actually are** (decades-old data standards, channel relationships, trade-association distribution). azFitment was invisible to me until verification; it's been obvious to every auto-parts seller for years. Unfamiliarity is not whitespace — it's the *opposite* signal in vertical markets. Specialized opportunities are found by domain insiders who feel the pain and know the incumbents' precise failures; outsiders find only the incumbents.

Which closes the loop on this entire engagement: **your specialized vertical is growth marketing itself** — the one domain where you are the insider who can tell adequate incumbents from broken ones. And that is exactly the territory the board already exploits: TrafficLens, AnswerRank, RateBook, GrowthReps are all insider reads of your own vertical. The specialized-areas directive, followed honestly, routes back to the board.
