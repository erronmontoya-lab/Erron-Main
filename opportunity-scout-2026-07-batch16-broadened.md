**The compounding detail:** CiteRate runs on the *same probing engine* as PromptShare and CreatorCited. The platform family is now **three corpora on one engine**: ChatGPT apps (builders) · YouTube citations (creators) · content citations (publishers). Each resurrection and survivor keeps converging on the same machine — the strongest possible signal about where the value sits.

Apps, creators, publishers — different buyers, one machine. At some point convergence stops being coincidence and starts being the answer.
