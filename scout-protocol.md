# Standing Scout Protocol — v1.0 (July 2026)

The monthly re-scan specification distilled from 15 batches, 111 searches, 34 scored ideas, and 75+ documented kills. Invoke with one message: **"Run the monthly scout."** Spends searches only where the calendar or a trigger says something may have changed. Full interactive rounds are retired unless a new platform shift occurs.

---

## 1. Constraint stack (current)

- Solo build / validate / scale; first revenue path ≤90 days; validation move #1 requires no code
- Platform-shaped preferred: Shape A (single-player tool → data platform) or Shape B (give-to-get network). Two-sided-liquidity-first plays are excluded
- Real durable value to the user; habit surface valued (daily/weekly recurring use)
- **Excluded:** compliance, healthcare, legal · anything whose mechanism is businesses-growing-through-connection (employer conflict, broad reading) · ideas requiring an existing audience · physical inventory/local ops · ad-spend-dependent growth

## 2. Kill gates (all five before any scorecard)

- **G1 Money-moving:** a named price is paid today for a workaround. Time-spent ≠ evidence. Law-firm hours count; free tools anchor against you.
- **G2 Embedded loop:** the loop lives in the product's output or usage mechanics. "Communities will share it" = fail.
- **G3 Incumbent radar:** funded company published on the exact problem within 6 months = circling = fail, unless the segment is orthogonal to their motion (price tier, buyer identity, or reputational distaste).
- **G4 90-day invoice:** collectible inside 90 days. A draft regulation, unshipped API, or unformed behavior = fail (watch-list it with a dated trigger instead).
- **G5 Platform hostage:** one platform's policy change kills it overnight *and* that platform has incentive → kill or hard-cap defensibility.

**Scoring rules:** 9 vectors, loop + saturation ×2, max 110, clear bar 75. No vector >6 without a citable fact; unproven assumptions cap at 5. **Two-search verification rule** (priors have failed in both directions: MarginDash existed; Ignition was alive). Check exemption thresholds in any demand thesis (MoCRA lesson). Re-verify effective dates the week of scoring (EUDR lesson). Adversarial re-score every survivor before it enters the board. Arbitrage theses require a *documented clearing spread*, not illiquidity (content-site lesson) and no professional buyer pool at the same tier (Shopify-apps lesson).
