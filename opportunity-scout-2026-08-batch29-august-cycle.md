## The Pay Per Use finding — the answer economy grows a cash register

Until now, AI-answer visibility was worth *traffic and brand*. Cloudflare's Pay Per Use makes appearance-in-AI-answers a **payable event** with a settlement system behind it. Two effects on the board:

1. **Measurement demand hardens.** When a quantity becomes a revenue line, measuring it stops being analytics and becomes accounting. The TrafficLens/AnswerRank measurement layer's long-term G1 case just upgraded from "marketers care about visibility" to "publishers will reconcile payouts against it."
2. **A new hypothesis falls out — and dies.** *CrawlLedger* (independent reconciliation/audit of publisher AI-crawl payments): killed at the gates. [Cloudflare runs the complete billing lifecycle itself — charge initiation, recording, aggregation, reconciliation, and monthly payouts](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-crawl-control/features/pay-per-crawl/use-pay-per-crawl-as-site-owner/manage-payouts/), and [TollBit already operates live per-crawl payment infrastructure](https://www.contentgrip.com/ai-publishers-crawl-fees/). G5 platform-hostage with the platform holding both the data and the incentive to keep reconciliation native. **Named killer: Cloudflare's own payout documentation.** The value of the trigger was never a new company — it was hardening the theses already on the board.
