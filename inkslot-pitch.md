# InkSlot — *Deposits first. No-shows gone.*

**The booking platform built for tattoo artists — not borrowed from salons.**

---

### The Problem
The average tattoo studio loses **$15,000–$40,000 a year to no-shows.** Artists cobble together Instagram DMs, a generic salon booking tool, and a separate payment app — none of which were built for how tattooers actually work. The tools that exist are salon-first retrofits with the wrong language, clunky basics, and — incredibly — **many can't even collect a deposit before confirming a booking.** Deposits are the one thing proven to fix the problem.

### The Solution
**InkSlot** is a tattoo-native booking platform where **taking a deposit is the default, not an afterthought.** A beautiful, artist-branded booking page collects a Stripe deposit *before* the slot is confirmed, sends automatic reminders, and keeps the artist's portfolio, consent, and aftercare forms in one place. Collecting a deposit at booking drops no-show rates from **20–30% to under 5%.** It pays for itself the first time it saves a single appointment.

### Why Now
- No dominant tattoo-native player exists — the market is fragmented salon retrofits (Fresha, Vagaro, GlossGenius) and small clunky tools. **White space, not a wall.**
- Stripe Connect + AI/no-code make a payments-native product buildable and operable by one person.
- Tattoo culture is Instagram-native and brand-obsessed — a gorgeous booking page is a feature artists will pay for and *show off*.

### Market
250,000+ tattoo artists and 20,000+ studios in the US alone, plus adjacent body-art, piercing, and boutique-artist verticals. High willingness to pay for tools that "get" their workflow ($30–150/mo range for niche pros).

### Business Model
- **Subscription:** ~$29–49/mo per artist.
- **Payments margin:** small cut on every deposit/payment processed via Stripe Connect — recurring revenue that grows with usage, no added acquisition cost.
- **Expansion:** consent forms, portfolios, rebooking, multi-artist studio plans.

### Distribution — the loop is built in
Every artist-branded booking page carries a subtle **"Powered by InkSlot"** and gets shared to Instagram Stories daily. Clients booking one artist discover the tool; artists broadcast to other artists constantly. **The product markets itself** — a Calendly-style watermark loop inside the most word-of-mouth-driven creative community there is.

### Moat
Tattoo-native depth generalists won't build (flash vs. custom deposit rules, guest-spot logistics, consent/aftercare), **embedded payments** that raise switching costs, and a compounding **brand + community** position as *the* artist tool.

### Why a Solo Founder Wins Here
Self-serve onboarding, weekly-use stickiness, low support burden, no compliance minefield — a genuinely one-person business. And it's a rare vertical where **marketing, brand, conversion, and product-led growth ARE the moat**, matching the founder's exact strengths instead of fighting them.

### The Plan (First 30 Days)
1. **Validate:** 20 artist interviews — confirm no-show cost + deposit frustration.
2. **Ship the wedge:** one branded booking page that takes a Stripe deposit before confirming. Nothing else.
3. **Land 5 paying artists**, capture their no-show drop as proof, and let the Instagram loop compound.

**Estimated probability of success: ~68% — the highest-scoring option across saturation, clarity of path, solo-operability, and founder fit.**
