## The near-miss — DataAct Kit: cloud-switching compliance for small SaaS — **74/110, one point under**

**What it is:** Contract-clause scanner + hosted "switching policy" page + data-export attestation for small SaaS providers caught by the EU Data Act's Chapter VI switching regime — [in force since September 12, 2025: mandatory switching terms in all contracts (including existing ones), max 2-month notice, switching charges banned entirely from January 12, 2027](https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2025/9/cloud-switching-under-the-eu-data-act).

**Why it misses:** three deductions that stricter scoring makes unavoidable. (1) Pain caps at 6 — enforcement is diffuse (national authorities, no private-right-of-action drama), so the pressure is procurement-driven and the evidence for *that* is thin so far. (2) [The Commission published free modular standard contractual clauses in November 2025](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/eu-data-act-what-businesses-need-to-know) — a free official template anchors the price of any paid template product at zero. (3) [The draft Digital Omnibus would ease the burden for SMEs on pre-existing contracts](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/eu-data-act-what-businesses-need-to-know) — the obligation may shrink mid-build.

**What would change the verdict:** any one of — (a) first national-authority enforcement action against a SaaS provider (pain 6→8, clears instantly); (b) evidence that enterprise procurement questionnaires now include Data Act switching-terms checks, the EAA/VPAT dynamic (pain 6→7, clears); (c) the Jan 12, 2027 total ban on switching charges approaching, which converts every SaaS contract with an egress-fee clause into a ticking violation (timing 7→9, clears). **Most likely resolution: fold it into DiscloseKit as SKU #3** — identical buyer (EU-exposed small SaaS), identical loop (hosted policy page + badge), shared regulatory-tracking spine. As a bundle component it doesn't need to clear the bar alone.
