# Startup Opportunity Scout — July 2026, Batch 17: The Corpus Sweep

Rationale: three independent lines (Batches 1, 11, 16) converged on one probing engine. The rational next batch was therefore systematic, not random — enumerate every remaining party who wins or loses when AI answers surface things, and test each as a corpus for the intent-share engine. 3 searches (116 cumulative), 3 corpora tested.

## ✅ Corpus 4 — Authors: VIABLE (guides-not-products verified)

The strongest possible signature: [Author Media publishes "Does ChatGPT Recommend Your Book?" as education](https://www.authormedia.com/does-chatgpt-recommend-your-book/); ["AI Visibility for Authors" guides](https://bemytech.com/ai-visibility-for-authors/) teach AIO tactics (trope-rich book pages, roundup placement); the industry consensus is that [AI conversations are the new word-of-mouth for book discovery — "you're either in the answer or you're invisible"](https://writelightgroup.com/2026/02/authors-guide-book-marketing-2026/). Guides everywhere. **No tracking product for authors found.**

Corpus profile: authors pay named prices for marketing tools (Publisher Rocket, BookBub, K-lytics — G1 ✓); author flex culture makes the badge/leaderboard loop *stronger* than any corpus yet ("Recommended by ChatGPT for [topic]" on a book page is irresistible); "best books on X" prompts are enumerable by genre. G3: enterprise GEO tools (Semrush/SE Ranking) price and position for SEO teams — authors are orthogonal. Standalone-equivalent score ≈ 77 (prosumer churn caveat, same as CreatorCited). **As corpus 4: excellent — arguably the best badge-loop economics of the five.**

## ⏸ Course creators: PREMATURE — parked with a trigger

Search found an ecosystem of AI course-*creation* tools and no "does ChatGPT recommend your course" content — **neither guides nor products.** Under this engagement's own rule, guides-not-products requires the guides: their absence means the pain isn't felt yet, not that it's unserved. Trigger to re-arm: first appearance of "get your course recommended by ChatGPT" content in creator-economy media.

## 🔀 Podcasts: absorbed brand-side; folded

The podcast-AI intersection is already framed for *brands*, not hosts: [podcast appearances generate 7–10 citation surfaces per episode, and category-authoritative guesting is an AEO tactic](https://evolveamz.com/podcast-youtube-citations-chatgpt-strategy/), with [SE Ranking/Semrush recommended for tracking](https://evolveamz.com/podcast-youtube-citations-chatgpt-strategy/). Host-side demand is thin; brand-side is absorbed into general GEO tooling. One salvage: **guesting-for-citations** ("5 authoritative appearances beat 20 low-authority ones") is a measurable service offer — folded into VertGEO's cash-engine menu, not a corpus.
