# Batch 27 — Disconfirmation Pass on the Diamond

Protocol rule (installed after Batch 23): every board leader gets a bear-case round before any build decision. GrowthReps — newly crowned the venture diamond — had only two searches of scrutiny. Three pre-registered bear cases; two searched (137 cumulative; third blocked by session quota and, it turns out, only answerable by validation anyway). **Verdict: survived, hardened, and re-scored upward — with one new risk and one roadmap change.**

## Bear case 1 — "The mechanic isn't novel; someone's already built it" → PARTIAL HIT that helps more than it hurts

The consulting-interview vertical has exactly GrowthReps' mechanic, mature and priced: [RocketBlocks sells timed drill practice (math, charts, structuring) at ~$155/year](https://www.rocketblocks.me/), [PrepLounge runs peer practice at 555,000+ users, and "AI-graded case practice became the new standard in 2025–26" with AI-native entrants like Road to Offer doing "AI-powered daily case reps with structured feedback"](https://www.roadtooffer.com/blog/best-case-interview-prep-tools-2026).

**What this kills:** the novelty claim. **What it proves:** everything else — G1 upgrades from analogy to *adjacent-vertical named prices* (professionals pay $155/yr for drill-based judgment practice; half a million use peer practice), and the AI-scoring mechanic is validated as buildable and buyable. **What it adds:** a real clock. AI-native practice platforms exist one vertical-pivot away from marketing. The marketing vertical re-verified empty under broader phrasing — but the window is now quarters, not years.

## Bear case 2 — "Prosumer business ed is a VC graveyard" → EXAMINED, and the graveyard explains itself

[Section4 raised big, laid off a quarter of staff in 2022 — leadership's stated reason: celebrity-professor courses were "too costly to produce"](https://techcrunch.com/2022/05/12/professor-scott-galloways-edtech-startup-section-4-lay-offs/) — then [survived by pivoting to certificates and 200+ enterprise clients](https://poetsandquantsforexecs.com/news/section4-rebrands-to-section-launches-new-certificates-for-business-professionals/). The autopsy is precise: **content-production COGS killed the category's economics — and AI just deleted that exact line item.** GrowthReps is a practice-loop, not a production studio; its scenario COGS approaches zero. The graveyard doesn't warn against this thesis; it *explains why the thesis is newly possible.* One lesson imported: survivors monetized B2B — **the team/enterprise tier moves from Phase 3 to Phase 2 on the roadmap** (practice leagues for marketing teams; L&D budgets are where Section found oxygen).

## Bear case 3 — "Nobody pays for practice without an interview" → OPEN, and now instrumented

The sharpest bear case stands unresolved: LeetCode, RocketBlocks, Exponent revenue is *interview-event-driven*; Duolingo runs on streak addiction. Whether employed professionals pay for judgment practice absent a job-search event is genuinely unproven — and no search can prove it. **So the validation sprint now carries the answer by design:** recruit the $29 cohort as half job-seekers, half employed marketers, and compare day-10 retention and willingness-to-continue between segments. If only job-seekers retain, GrowthReps is "marketing interview prep" (a real but smaller business, Exponent-adjacent); if employed marketers retain, the venture thesis holds. One experiment, both futures priced.

## Re-score and board effect

Solo rubric: Pain 6→7 (adjacent-vertical WTP proven at named prices) → **GrowthReps 78→79.** Venture memo: thesis *de-risked* (mechanic proven adjacent, graveyard explained, COGS killer removed) with urgency raised (AI-native entrants near). The diamond survived the same treatment that knocked PromptShare down four points and TrafficLens down six — which is exactly what being a diamond means.

**Updated cluster:** RateBook 81 · PromptShare 80 (gated) · **GrowthReps 79 ↑ (venture diamond, diligence-hardened)** · ListReclaim 79 · CreatorCited 78 · CiteRate 77. The two-week sprint is no longer just validation — it's now a designed experiment that resolves the last open bear case, prices both the bootstrap and venture futures, and starts the clock on an empty vertical with proven mechanics and visible neighbors. That's as close to "the best idea, verified" as any search can carry you. The rest is the doing.
