## Channel verdict 1 — Gig mining: validated as a demand instrument; one paradox worth watching

The channel works — it surfaces what buyers pay humans for *repeatedly*, which is the rawest WTP data available:

- [AI-related freelance demand +109% YoY; AI video generation/editing +329% — the fastest-growing skill on Upwork](https://bestjobsearchapps.com/articles/en/most-indemand-freelance-skills-for-2026-ai-and-data-dominate-upwork-and-fiverr)
- [A single client needs 20–40 short-form clips per month at $40–150/video; podcast editing runs $100–300/episode; top sellers build five-figure months on retainers](https://unil.ink/blog/best-fiverr-gigs-2026)

**The clip-economy paradox, recorded as a watch item:** demand for *human* AI-video editors grew 329% in a market where AI clipping tools (Opus Clip, Vizard — killed in Batch 1 triage as saturated) already exist. Growth of human services *despite* saturated tooling means the tools' quality ceiling is below buyer requirements. That's not a solo opportunity today (the incumbents are funded and iterating; the services demand is a services business) — but it defines a dated-by-capability trigger: **when model quality closes the gap the current tools can't, a next-generation slot opens against incumbents anchored to old architectures.** Undated; model-dependent; on the watch list.

## Channel verdict 2 — G2 category formation: structurally lagging, reclassified

The hope: [16 new categories reaching Grid eligibility per quarter](https://www.g2.com/best-software-companies/fastest-growing) = slots being born. The reality, from G2's own methodology: **Grid eligibility requires 6+ products with 10+ reviews each** — meaning by the time this channel shows a category, it is *occupied by definition*. The named examples confirm it (ASPM: enterprise security; AI Consulting: services). And the velocity data doubles as a warning: [AEO went from new-category debut to top-growth with 12 products added in a single quarter](https://company.g2.com/news/g2-summer-2026-reports) — money-adjacent categories now saturate inside 90 days.

**Reclassification:** G2 category formation is a *kill-confirmer*, not a gem-finder — added to the standing routine as the quarterly saturation check, removed as a sourcing channel. (Also noted: G2 and PR Newswire block direct page fetches — primary-source mining there needs the summaries.)
