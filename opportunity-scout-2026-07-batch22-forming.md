## Forming Wave 2 — The AI inbox gatekeeping era: **email's measurement layer is breaking now**

[Gmail's Gemini-3 integration (January 2026, default-on) made the inbox an active gatekeeper: AI Overviews summarize threads, relevance-based sorting deprioritizes marketing email that clears spam but fails the AI's relevance threshold](https://adpulse.com/gemini-ai-in-gmail-google-updates-email-marketing/). Measured impact already: [industry CTR fell from ~4.35% to ~3.93%, and open rates are now unreliable because Gmail auto-opens emails to generate summaries](https://emailexpert.com/gmail-launches-ai-inbox/).

**What's forming:** two products, not one. The InboxLens thesis (summary simulation) is accelerating — but the bigger forming wedge is **email metrics integrity**: when AI auto-opens poison open rates, every lifecycle marketer's dashboards lie, exactly as GA4's did when agent traffic arrived. That is *TrafficLens's thesis transplanted to email* — the founder's home turf, with the same "guides forming, products absent" curve about 12 months behind the web-analytics version. **Protocol upgrade:** the InboxLens watch item (Jan 2027) expands in scope to "email measurement integrity + summary simulation" and gains urgency; the Litmus-shipping check now covers both angles.
