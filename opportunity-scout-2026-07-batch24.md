## Finding 1 — WebMCP: the agent-readiness watch item is now DATED, and it's the anti-app-store play

Batch 23 found brands hesitant to hand customer relationships to OpenAI's app store. The alternative architecture just got a calendar: [WebMCP — the W3C-incubated standard (Google + Microsoft co-authored) letting websites expose tools to agents on their own domain — shipped Chrome 146 developer preview in February, hits origin trial in Chrome 149, with Edge implementation and enterprise crossover expected late 2026 into 2027](https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/webmcp-google-browser-agent-standard-2026). [Expedia, Booking.com, Shopify, Target, Etsy, Instacart are already piloting](https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/webmcp-google-browser-agent-standard-2026). The guides are forming *right now* ([WebMCP for marketers](https://wrodium.com/blogs/webmcp-for-marketing), [tool-defined lead capture](https://www.leadgen-economy.com/blog/webmcp-tool-defined-lead-capture/)) — the pre-category curve at its steepest.

**Placement:** not buildable for 90-day revenue today (origin trial ≠ deployed demand — G4 fails *now*), but this is Batch 1's AgentGrade thesis and Batch 9's AnswerLand conversion layer maturing on schedule: "agent-facing lead capture beside the web form" is a growth marketer's product if anything ever was. **Actions:** watch trigger updated from undated to *Chrome 149 origin trial → Edge ship → first enterprise deployments (late 2026)*; land-bank position #2 registered behind Siri (domain + "WebMCP for marketers" pillar content + waitlist — one weekend).

## Finding 2 — Franchise development: the engine's first B2B-priced corpus candidate (flagged)

The engine's persistent weakness is prosumer unit economics ($10–30/mo, churny). Franchise development inverts it: [65% of franchise leads now originate from digital, prospective franchisees are consulting ChatGPT/Perplexity/Claude directly, and AI-sourced leads arrive more educated and further down-funnel](https://biziq.com/blog/franchise-marketing-statistics-2026/) — against $10K+/lead franchise-development economics. Bonus data-hygiene pain: [only 68% of business contact info in AI answers matches Google Business Profiles, so franchise systems are being misrepresented or omitted](https://ignitevisibility.com/ai-seo-franchise-sales/). "Best franchise under $200K" is a natural, enumerable, high-value prompt space.

**The flag:** the vertical's marketing is agency-dominated and the agencies are already circling with content ([Cheers' GEO academy for franchises](https://www.cheers.tech/geo-academy/how-franchise-brands-get-recommended-by-chatgpt), [Ignite Visibility selling AI-SEO for franchise sales](https://ignitevisibility.com/ai-seo-franchise-sales/)). A product sold direct to franchisors fights the agencies; sold *through* them (white-label franchise-visibility reports on the engine), it rides them. **Placement:** Corpus #8 — B2B tier ($500–2K/mo equivalent), agency-channel motion, G3-flagged. Parked with a white-label lens; not the wedge.
