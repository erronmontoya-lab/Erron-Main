## The process finding: priors fail in both directions

My pre-registered prediction for launch-management tooling was a *demand graveyard* — I believed Ignition (the funded GTM-launch platform) had shut down, which would have killed the category on "funded attempt died" grounds. Verification found the opposite: [Ignition is alive, serving 800+ teams, with active 2025 reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/ignition-gtm/reviews). The hypothesis still dies — but as **occupied territory (G3)**, not as a graveyard. Combined with the MarginScope case (assumed-empty → found-occupied), the two-search verification rule has now caught wrong priors in both directions: assumed-dead-actually-alive and assumed-open-actually-taken. No score gets finalized on memory. Ever.
