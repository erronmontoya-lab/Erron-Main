# Final Evaluation — The Opportunity Above the Board

Directive: evaluate everything held; find the opportunity that tops the best (PromptShare, 84). Method: score-cap analysis of the full portfolio, then an honest test of whether any construction the evidence supports can legitimately exceed 84 — under the same gates that killed a hundred ideas.

---

## Part 1 — What 19 batches actually assembled

122 searches. 40+ scored ideas. ~100 documented kills with named killers. Four sherlock-risk predictions that came true on schedule (Replit/Lovable→VibeCheck, Google→TrafficLens, Stripe→MCP Meter). And a board whose top entries are not interchangeable ideas but **distinct archetypes**, each maxing different vectors:

| Archetype | Best specimen | What it maxes | What caps it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tracker/data platform | PromptShare 84 | Saturation 9, Timing 9, Loop 8 | Pain 6 (young buyer urgency), Defensibility 6 (platform whim) |
| Product line | TrafficLens+AnswerLand 82+77 | Fit 10, buyer-credibility | Feature risk (Def 5), loop not fully product-emitted |
| Give-to-get network | RateBook 81 | Loop 9, Defensibility 7–8 | Episodic habit surface, two-sided ramp (Econ 6) |
| Arbitrage | ListReclaim 79 | Econ 8, day-one revenue | Pain 5 (no pain, only spread), no venture ceiling |
| Founder-DNA product | GrowthReps 78 | Fit 10 (product *is* the founder) | Prosumer churn (Econ 6), slow status-moat |

**The empirical finding on "topping 84":** five independent search directions — hypothesis-first, channel rotation, resurrection review, corpus sweep, creative generation — each terminated at or below the board. Nothing *searchable* tops 84, and that's now a measured fact, not a feeling. If something higher exists, it must come not from finding but from **composition**: combining what maxes each vector into one construction, and testing whether the synergy is real or self-deception.

## Part 2 — The composition test

The engagement's three deepest findings compose:

1. **The convergent engine** (Batches 1, 11, 16, 17): probe AI surfaces → attribute who gets surfaced → longitudinal rank data → leaderboards and badges. Three independent lines converged on it; a fourth (authors) verified as its best loop carrier.
2. **The prosumer-tail thesis** (Batch 17): builders, creators, publishers, and authors are one underserved market in four costumes — named-price tool budgets, flex-driven loops, enumerable prompt spaces — below the altitude Profound/Semrush will ever serve.
3. **The measurement→action lesson** (Batch 9): trackers alone churn ("audit-and-leave"); pairing measurement with an *action layer* converts diagnosis into retained workflow. And the founder's signature skill — habit formation — is precisely the discipline that turns "check your rank" into "do today's three actions."

## Part 3 — The opportunity that tops the best one

### **AnswerRank** (working name): the visibility operating system for everyone the AI answer economy pays or starves

**What it is:** One engine, four sequential markets, two layers. The **measurement layer** is PromptShare as specified — intent-share tracking, leaderboards, badges — launched on Corpus 1 (ChatGPT apps, the emptiest field with the lit monetization fuse). The **action layer** is what no pure tracker has: a daily practice surface — "three actions today to win the prompts you're losing" — with streaks, skill graphs, and before/after proof, built with GrowthReps' habit mechanics. Sequenced corpus expansion: apps → YouTube creators → niche publishers → authors, each inheriting the engine, the badge economy, and the playbook library.

**Why it scores above every component — vector by vector, honestly:**

| Vector | Score | Why it beats the parts |
|---|---|---|
| Pain | 7 | Validated across four buyer types independently — including publishers rich enough to build it themselves (Future, Forbes) — not one young segment's anxiety |
| Timing | 9 | Corpus 1's window is verified open with the OpenAI-monetization fuse lit (Cycle 1 trigger check) |
| AI exposure | 7 | Engine is AI-enabled; corpus data is AI-proof |
| **Loop (2x)** | **8** | Badge flex across four flex-cultures (authors strongest found in 19 batches) + leaderboard citation loop + the action layer's shareable win-streaks |
| **Saturation (2x)** | **8** | Empty in the tail across *all four* corpora (each verified separately); enterprise players structurally can't follow down-market |
| Solo | 7 | One engine, ~70% reuse per corpus; sequenced GTM keeps it one person's company for years |
| Fit | 9 | Distribution data-PR (your craft) + habit product design (your signature) in one artifact |
| Unit econ | 8 | Four stacked GTMs on shared infra; the action layer converts tracker-churn into workflow-retention — the single biggest LTV lever available |
| Defensibility | 8 | The step-change: **cross-corpus longitudinal recommendation data** — the only entity holding rank history across apps, videos, content, and books — plus four badge networks. No single-corpus competitor can backfill it; no enterprise player wants to |

**Weighted total: 87/110** — the highest score of the engagement, and the only one built entirely from independently verified components rather than a single bet.

### The discipline clause (read this twice)

Synergy premiums are where founders lie to themselves, so the same gates apply: **the 87 is a roadmap score, contingent on the 84 wedge validating first.** If Corpus 1's fifteen builders won't pay $99/month for tracking, the composite doesn't exist — no amount of platform vision rescues an unvalidated wedge. Nothing about the sprint changes; only the size of what it's testing changed. The kill criteria stand exactly as written in the final-selection doc: <5 founding customers by week 6, or OpenAI ships native invocation analytics first → fall back to TrafficLens/AnswerLand, which remains the fully independent second archetype.

### What could top 87

Nothing findable — that's now measured across five search modalities. Above 87 there is only *earned* score: validation data raising Pain from 7 to evidence-backed 8+, shipped corpora raising Defensibility from 8 to 9. The board above the board isn't discovered. It's built.
