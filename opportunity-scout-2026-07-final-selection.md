# Final Selection — July 2026

Selection criteria confirmed by founder: (1) buildable, validatable, and scalable by one person; (2) ideally a platform; (3) must drive real, durable value to the audience using it. Combined with standing constraints: no compliance/healthcare/legal, no growth-through-connection mechanics (employer conflict), no ideas requiring an existing audience.

## The structural insight: "platform" + "solo" only combine two ways

Two-sided platforms need liquidity before they deliver value — the one thing a solo founder cannot brute-force. The solo-viable platform shapes are:

- **Shape A — tool→data-platform:** single-player value on day one; the aggregate data becomes the network effect over time (Sensor Tower, Levels.fyi, G2 origin stories).
- **Shape B — give-to-get data network:** contribution is the signup, so liquidity self-assembles (Glassdoor mechanic).

Everything else on the board is a tool, a service, or an arbitrage.

## The selection: PromptShare, framed as the platform it actually is

**The platform arc (Shape A):**
- **Phase 1 — tool (months 0–6):** intent-share rank tracking for ChatGPT-app builders. Single-player value from customer #1: "which prompts invoke my app vs. competitors, and what changed this week." Daily-check habit surface — your adoption expertise applied to product design, not just marketing.
- **Phase 2 — suite (months 6–18):** the optimization layer on top of the data — intent-gap recommendations, metadata testing, category benchmarks. The Helium10/Semrush arc: tracking is the wedge, workflows are the retention.
- **Phase 3 — platform (18+):** the operating surface where builders manage distribution across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini app surfaces. Data network effects fully engaged: more builders → more probe coverage → better benchmarks → the platform every new builder must join. Optional second side later (brands/agencies buying category intelligence), added only after single-sided liquidity exists.

**Against the three criteria:**
1. *Solo build/validate/scale:* probing infra is cron jobs + LLM API calls; validation is 15 DMs and one leaderboard post; scaling is self-serve SaaS with light support. The public app directory is a named prospect universe — no audience required (your structural constraint).
2. *Platform:* Shape A with a compounding, non-backfillable longitudinal dataset — the only idea across ten batches that lost zero points to the elite gates.
3. *Real value:* builders get discovered and win distribution; end users get better apps surfaced for their intent. Nobody is being sold fear — the value is growth delivered, which is also the work you find meaningful.

**Score: 84/110 (elite, unchanged across three verification passes). Risk to respect:** OpenAI shipping native analytics — the mitigation (neutral cross-platform aggregation with competitor visibility) is structural, and Phase 2/3 workflow depth is the escape velocity.

## Runner-up: RateBook (Shape B) — with the habit fix that makes it viable

RateBook is the truest *platform* on the board (two-sided data network, best loop of all 31 scored ideas) but was downgraded for episodic usage. The fix, credited to your habit lens: **attach to deal cadence, not repricing cadence.** Agencies reprice annually but *quote weekly* — so the product isn't "check the benchmark," it's a per-proposal pricing calculator backed by live market data. Every quote is a session; every session contributes data. That converts the board's weakest habit surface into a workflow product. Hold it as the pivot destination if PromptShare's platform risk materializes — the give-to-get engine is buildable by the same person with the same skills.

## What the criteria eliminate

- **ListReclaim** — sound arbitrage, but it's buying, not building, and it's a portfolio, not a platform. Keep only as an optional later distribution acquisition (buy the newsletter your platform's ICP reads), not as the venture.
- **TrafficLens/AnswerLand** — excellent product line, deepest peer credibility, but a tool set, not a platform; no compounding data moat comparable to PromptShare's. Fallback if PromptShare validation fails.
- **CreatorRetain** — a service. **FeedFixer** — a feature with a platform-absorption clock. **PluginReclaim/ExtReclaim** — arbitrage. All retired from the primary track.

## The 90-day validation plan (PromptShare)

1. **Week 1–2 (no code):** DM 15 builders with live directory apps — one question: "What would you pay to know your invocation share vs. competitors for your top 20 intents?" Simultaneously, manually probe 50 high-intent prompts in one category.
2. **Week 3:** publish "Who wins [category] prompts in ChatGPT — August 2026" as the first leaderboard; measure builder inbound and waitlist conversion.
3. **Week 4–6:** pre-sell a $99/mo founding tier to 10 builders off the leaderboard's traffic. 10 paying = build Phase 1. Fewer than 5 = run the same play on TrafficLens (the fallback shares the validation muscle, not the code).
4. **Week 7–12:** ship the tracker to founding customers; weekly leaderboard becomes the standing content loop; every rank change is a retention event.

Kill criteria, stated now: <5 founding customers by week 6, or OpenAI ships builder-facing invocation analytics before Phase 1 revenue — in either case, pivot to the fallback without sunk-cost negotiation.
